# 土豆战神素材生成记录

参考：项目根目录 `土豆战神.png`，仅作为角色与服装美术参考。采用内置 imagegen；保存原始 alpha PNG，运行时按单元格取帧，不对角色抠色或重绘。

20 款皮肤，各自具有 100 / 75 / 50 / 25 四档，共 80 帧。每张皮肤图集为四行四列，行对应皮肤，列对应完整、损耗、胶带、纸箱。第六张为道具图集。

## 图集 1：armor-1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent PNG game sprite atlas. Generate a SINGLE square 2048x2048 atlas of exactly 16 separate full-body desktop pet sprites, in a STRICT 4 columns by 4 rows equal-size grid. Reference image is character and costume inspiration only. Match the cute detailed 3D toy render: happy tan round potato face with tiny freckles, glossy black eyes, little mouth and blush cheeks emerging from a chunky miniature mech helmet. Full body including both feet, all weapons are harmless little toys. Studio warm soft light, no floor, no shadow outside sprite. ACTUAL transparent alpha background, not a checkerboard drawing, no colored backdrops. NO labels, NO numbers, NO frames, NO letters. Each sprite centered in its exact equal cell with generous 10 percent empty gutter, whole silhouette entirely inside cell. Consistent orthographic front-three-quarter camera, same character height, feet baseline and scale in all cells. Critical: EVERY row is a different specified skin. EVERY row has FOUR quota degradation stages from left to right. Col1: pristine full complete spectacular detailed armor. Col2: mildly scratched armor with one small loose/damaged armor plate, still complete silhouette and identifiable skin, confident potato face. Col3: scrappy battered armor repaired with obvious beige adhesive tape strips and some exposed cardboard patches, retaining skin's distinct helmet and main colors, determined smiling potato. Col4: funny cardboard box torso mech and short cardboard limbs, potato head exposed but retains a small identifying version of this skin's helmet/hat/crest, taped-up improvised cardboard version of skin's shield/accessory, still confident and cute. Must be 16 DIFFERENT full-body sprites, no duplicates and no missing characters. For ALL 4 columns keep each row's costume identity.
Rows top to bottom:
Row1: white blue red RX-style Gundam armor with golden V crest, broad shoulders and rectangular red shield.
Row2: bulky red gold Iron Man armor, open gold faceplate around potato face, cyan circular chest reactor.
Row3: bulky blue Captain America armor, A helmet and white chest star, large circular red white blue shield.
Row4: Batman black armored bat-eared cowl framing potato face, gold belt, short cape, bat shaped chest plate.
